# Training

## When to use it

Use the Training plugin tools when the user asks about one of these:

- How to set up or perform a named exercise (for example a goblet squat, a Romanian deadlift, a push-up or a pull-up).
- Which muscles an exercise works, as primary and secondary muscles.
- Common mistakes to avoid, or general safety notes, for an exercise.
- Exercises for a muscle or movement pattern (squat, hinge, lunge, horizontal or vertical push and pull, carry, core, isolation).
- A substitute for an exercise, at home or in a gym, or exercises that can be done with no equipment or with home-friendly equipment.
- What a piece of equipment is, what it is typically used for, setup tips, safety notes and alternatives.

## What it returns

Each exercise entry has: an id and name, a category, a level (for example beginner, intermediate or advanced), the equipment setups it can be done with (each setup is a complete set of items; separate setups are alternatives; an empty list means no equipment), primary and secondary muscles, setup steps, execution steps, common mistakes, safety notes, and home and gym substitutions given as exercise ids.

Each equipment entry has: an id and name, a type, the muscles it is usually used to train (or, for the foam roller, the areas it is used on), its typical use, setup tips, common mistakes, safety notes, whether it is home-friendly, and alternative equipment ids.

Every response ends with a short note to talk to a doctor before starting to lift or exercise. Keep that note when you relay the answer.

## Limits

- **General reference only.** The entries describe exercises in general terms. They are not a training program, and they are not matched to any person's age, body, history or goals.
- **No personal or medical advice.** Do not use these tools to diagnose pain or injury, decide whether an exercise is safe for a specific condition, or plan rehabilitation. If the user describes pain, an injury, a health condition, pregnancy or a recent surgery, say that this needs a doctor or a qualified professional, and do not choose exercises for them from the library.
- **No product recommendations.** General reference only: it gives no personal or medical advice and no product recommendations. Do not use it to choose, rank or compare products or stores.
- **Authored data only.** Coverage is the authored set of exercises and equipment entries that the tools return. Nutrition, supplements, diets, weight-loss targets, sets-and-reps prescriptions and sports-specific coaching are outside the data. If an exercise or piece of equipment is not in the library, say so rather than filling the gap with an invented entry.
- **Read-only.** The tools do not store anything about the user and do not require a login.
